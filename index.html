RoxPB

Ahead or behind? One button tells you.

RoxPB is a Garmin watch app for HYROX-format races, simulations and training. Build a plan from your course, your running pace and a target for each station. Press LAP at every checkpoint and the watch shows how far ahead or behind you are.

Three ways to train

Race, Sim / Training or Run

Pick one each time you open the app. Race paces your event against your plan. Sim / Training builds a session from any runs and stations. Run paces intervals, threshold, hills and continuous runs by pace, speed or heart rate, outdoors or on a treadmill.

Plan your race

See what your pace gets you

Set your running pace and a time for each of the eight stations, and RoxPB works out your estimated finish. Change one number and the estimate moves with it. Or open a past result and choose "Use these settings" to start from a race that went well.

After the finish

Every split, on your phone

Open any result from your watch to see the full breakdown: each run and station against your plan and your best, with heart rate, plus your run, station and Roxzone totals. Save it as an image to keep or share.

The result travels inside the link and is drawn on your own phone. Nothing is stored.
